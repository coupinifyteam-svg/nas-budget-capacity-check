Three budgets to compare

These examples use illustrative prices in U.S. dollars. Add sales tax, delivery, and any accessories separately before you order.

One 4 TB drive

A $280 enclosure and one $200 drive cost $480. Against a $700 budget, the drive leaves $500 available for the enclosure. Usable capacity is about 4 TB before overhead.

Two 4 TB drives in RAID 1

The same enclosure and two $200 drives cost $680. The drives leave $300 available for the enclosure. The pair has 8 TB combined and about 4 TB usable before overhead.

The drives exceed the budget

Two $180 drives cost $360. With a $300 budget, the drives alone are $60 over. Revise the budget or price a different pair before choosing an enclosure.

Use the JavaScript estimator

Call estimateNas with prices in whole cents and drive capacity in decimal TB. Use basic for one drive or raid1 for two identical drives. The function returns costs in cents, raw and usable capacity in TB, and usable capacity in TiB.

import { estimateNas } from "./nas-budget.mjs";

const result = estimateNas({
  budgetCents: 70000,
  enclosureCents: 28000,
  pricePerDriveCents: 20000,
  capacityPerDriveTB: 4,
  mode: "raid1",
});

console.log(result.subtotalCents); // 68000
console.log(result.enclosureBudgetCents); // 30000
console.log(result.usableTB); // 4
How the numbers are calculated

The hardware subtotal adds the enclosure and drive costs. The available enclosure budget is the hardware budget minus the cost of the drives, with a floor of $0. For a two-drive mirror, usable capacity equals the capacity of one drive before formatting and system overhead. To convert TB to TiB, multiply by 1012 and divide by 240.

Check the compatibility list for the exact NAS and drive model. Keep a recoverable backup separate from the NAS.
